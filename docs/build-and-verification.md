## Local evidence

The original local refresh completed across the 28-instrument universe. Restart
persistence and isolated restore were accepted later with retained private
reports and backup checksum verification. Those private reports and backups do
not enter this showcase.

After the makeover rebuild, a coordinator walkthrough loaded 28 saved instruments
and four observed scan runs. It inspected an instrument's stored evidence,
closed its drawer with Escape and verified focus return, selected an older run,
returned to latest, and opened Strategy Guide. No new provider scan was requested
during that walkthrough.

## Limits of the evidence

Synthetic captures use authored example observations in the actual React
components. The six selected images document those examples, not live quotes.
Cloud Chromium checks do not establish Safari/iOS acceptance or a hosted service.
The local walkthrough did not repeat the full suite or database restore.
No measured time saved, trading outcome or backtest validity is claimed.
